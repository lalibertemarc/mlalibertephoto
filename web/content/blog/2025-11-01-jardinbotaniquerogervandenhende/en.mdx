This summer, I treated myself to a new lens, namely the [Sony FE 16mm F1.8 G](https://www.sony.ca/en/electronics/camera-lenses/sel16f18g).

I went for a walk in the [Roger-Van den Hende Botanical Garden](https://www.jardin.ulaval.ca/en/) of Université Laval to practice and have fun with the lens.

Here are some photos of the main building that I like. I gave them a cold tint which really highlights the flowers.
