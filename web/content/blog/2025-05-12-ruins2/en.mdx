The ruins of the Montmills debarking mill, located near Lac du Délaissé in Beauport. <br />
This mill was in operation from 1908 to 1947 and was destroyed by fire on June 25, 1943. <br />
The pillar seen here supported the slab that transported the logs to the Montmorency River. <br />
